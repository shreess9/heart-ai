# Responsible AI & Ethical Framework

H.E.A.R.T. AI operates on strict human-centered, reliability-aware principles.

---

## 1. Non-Diagnostic Constraint
- **Core Principle**: The system is an interactive companion, **not** a clinical diagnostic tool.
- **Rules**:
  - The system must NEVER diagnose depression, anxiety, cognitive decline, dementia, or any psychiatric or somatic condition.
  - The system must NEVER present emotional inference as ground truth about a person's inner psychological state.
  - Emotional inferences are statistical approximations of expressive cues, not internal facts.

---

## 2. Epistemic Humility & Reliability-Aware Action
- When modality signals disagree or uncertainty is high, the system must reduce the strength of affective adaptation.
- The system must provide conversational space for the user to clarify, self-correct, or steer the dialogue.
- Presumptive claims (e.g., *"You are feeling depressed today"*) are strictly prohibited.

---

## 3. Privacy, Consent & Modality Autonomy
- **Informed Consent**: Users are fully informed about how audio, text, and visual signals are analyzed.
- **Modality Autonomy**: Users can disable camera or microphone at any time without degrading the remaining capabilities.
- **Data Minimization**: Media streams are processed in memory and immediately discarded. No continuous recording or unauthorized persistent video storage.

---

## 4. Caregiver Dashboard Principles
- **Support, Not Surveillance**: Caregiver insights present high-level, longitudinal engagement patterns and ambiguous interaction summaries.
- **No Pathologizing Language**: Summaries must avoid pathologizing everyday emotional variation or framing statistical emotion estimates as medical status.
