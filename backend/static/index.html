H.E.A.R.T. AI Technical Architecture & Pipeline Flow

7-Stage Technical Architecture

The core innovation of H.E.A.R.T. AI is decoupling emotion classification from reliability-gated response adaptation. The companion does not simply adapt to raw predictions; it evaluates cross-modal disagreement and confidence before selecting a conversational action tier.

Stage 1 Older Adult Conversation (Single MP4 Media Container) Automatic Decomposition • Video frames (24 fps) • 16kHz mono audio waveform
↓
Stage 2 Pretrained Modality Encoders Whisper ASR → RoBERTa (Text) • Wav2Vec 2.0 (Audio SER) • ViT (Visual Face FER)
↓
Stage 3 Modality Emotion Evidence Distributions P_text • P_audio • P_visual (7-Class Categorical Probability Distributions)
↓
Stage 4A Multimodal Fusion Layer Dynamic Early Fusion • Calibration
→ Predicted Emotion + Confidence
Stage 4B Cross-Modal Disagreement Engine D₁: Generalized JSD • D₂: Pairwise • D₃: Categorical
→ Cross-Modal Incongruity Signal
↓
Stage 5 Reliability Assessment Engine Fuses Confidence + D₁ + D₃ + Entropy → Reliability Level: HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW
↓
Stage 6 Reliability Gate & Adaptive Conversational Policy • HIGH → Strong Emotion Adaptation
• MEDIUM → Cautious / Tentative Adaptation
• LOW → Neutral Supportive / Clarification Query
↓
Stage 7 H.E.A.R.T. AI Companion Response Generation (Gemini LLM) Conversational grounding tailored for older adult companionship with zero clinical intrusion

Key Research Discovery on MELD:

On MELD dialogues, cross-modal disagreement (D₁ ≈ 0.55) peaks when Text is correct and Audio/Visual sensors fail (T₁_A₀_V₀). Confidence alone fails to intercept these errors due to overconfidence in text representations. Disagreement-aware gating intercepts 693 emotion misclassifications (81.6% of all baseline errors) and routes conflicting samples to Clarification queries.

Future Dataset & Modality Scope:

Extension to older-adult-focused conversational datasets such as ElderReact is planned for future work, subject to dataset availability, licensing, and multimodal sensor compatibility. Additional directions include real-world noisy audio/video robustness, asynchronous modality arrival, personalized response adaptation thresholds, and longitudinal affective attunement.

Responsible AI, Privacy & Clinical Scope

Ethical Governance
👨‍⚕️

1. No Medical or Clinical Diagnosis

H.E.A.R.T. AI is designed exclusively as an empathetic conversational companion for older adults. It does NOT diagnose depression, cognitive decline, anxiety, or any medical/psychiatric condition.

🔒

2. Strict Opt-In Privacy

Audio recording and camera inputs are strictly opt-in. Raw audio waveforms and video frames should not be retained beyond immediate turn inference unless explicitly consented.

⚠️

3. Uncertainty-First Caution

Emotion prediction is inherently uncertain and noisy in natural elder conversations. When multimodal evidence is conflicting or unconfident, the companion defaults to neutral supportive grounding.

⚙️

4. User Autonomy & Control

Users and caregivers can disable emotion-aware adaptation at any time, returning the companion to standard non-adaptive conversational dialogue.
